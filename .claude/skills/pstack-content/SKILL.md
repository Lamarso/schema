# Content and creative verification

Use the store's current brief, brand guidance, audience, channel and approved offers. Keep domain experts and existing creative skills in charge of the craft.

Establish the intended audience action and the few constraints that determine acceptance: language, format, dimensions, duration, placement, product identity or required text. Use the actual catalog and approved source for prices, discounts, availability, product specifications and calendar claims. Treat unsourced performance statistics and legal/compliance assertions as claims to verify, not reusable facts.

Generate alternatives when the brief has genuine creative uncertainty; choose against the brief rather than producing a large panel by default. Remove repetitive or generic copy while preserving necessary qualifications and useful explanation.

Preview or render the actual output in the relevant surface. Check the relevant mobile/desktop crop, text legibility, encoding, links, placeholders and product-to-asset correspondence. For video, inspect representative frames and timing/audio where available. A generated file or valid HTML alone does not prove the audience sees the intended result.

Use existing preview/test-send controls within their authorization. Preparing an asset is separate from publishing, sending or buying traffic. Report what was previewed and what still needs platform verification. When evaluating results, use `pstack-metrics` rather than treating aesthetic preference as a measured lift.
