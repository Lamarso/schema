# Business P-stack

Use the existing project runbook, role, tools and owner authorizations. This skill supplies a work method; it grants no new permissions and does not replace domain skills.

1. Identify the requested outcome and an observable acceptance check. For a small task this can stay implicit; avoid a planning ceremony.
   Restate the goal: in your first message, at the top of each delegated brief and at the top of the final report, say in your own words what the owner wants and the problem it solves. If the evidence rules out the requested change, show that evidence and stop rather than doing it anyway. Skip this for a fully specified one-line edit.
2. Inspect the relevant source, current state and existing checks before changing behavior. Distinguish observed facts, assumptions and missing evidence. Investigate causes before adding guards or workarounds.
3. Make the smallest coherent change that meets the goal. Split a broad task into independently verifiable units. Delegate independent research or review when it materially helps; give each worker a scope and evidence requirement. Prefer isolated independent targets; assign one writer to each shared mutable resource or UI session.
4. Check the user-visible result using the role's actual surface. A successful API response, compilation or an agent's confidence is insufficient evidence of the business outcome. Use safe fixtures or existing test modes for external effects; do not trigger real sends, spending, refunds or publishing merely to demonstrate a check.
5. Report the result, evidence and remaining limits. Say whether a change is local, proposed, registered or verified in production. Do not present an unrun check as passing.

Load only the module needed: `pstack-engineering`, `pstack-operations`, `pstack-content`, `pstack-metrics`, or `pstack-state-model`. For mixed work use the relevant combination. Preserve existing approval boundaries and standing authorizations. Skill adoption is not authority to message people, merge, deploy or widen access.

When a failure recurs, prefer a small executable check or a correction to the existing runbook over another layer of generic instructions. Keep evidence free of credentials and unnecessary customer data.
