Earlier: P-stack 0.15.32, initially reviewed at `9580c23696ac8d7abed0c166f2f9918800afb819` and checked again at `99b609a72323b70204eab407c622f436e858e112` on 25 October 2026.

Adapted from 0.15.33-0.15.36: restate the goal (pstack-business), decide before you measure (pstack-metrics) and a `/statechart` pointer for many-state flows (pstack-state-model).

Source: https://github.com/cursor/plugins/tree/1ba91fc25c0977741dd0e267849e6234b037ed0a/pstack

Adapted ideas include observable verification, root-cause investigation, behavior tests, feature maps, explaining measurements, verifiable units, selective independent review, structural learning and conditional state modeling. The wording and business-role routing here are locally authored.

Excluded from automatic installation: upstream model defaults, external-action authority, mandatory multi-model swarms, PR auto-merge, overnight loops, transcript mining, notification automations, blanket comment removal and unrelated personal style. Existing project policies and owner authorizations remain authoritative.

The upstream MIT license is included in `LICENSE.pstack` for attribution. Automatic maintenance checks the official source weekly on Mondays at 09:00 Europe/Stockholm while the Mac and Codex app are running. It reviews relevant changes, evaluates warranted adaptations, and applies qualified updates to registered local copies with backups and conflict checks. Corresponding repository changes are published as draft PRs; hosted activation remains a separate release. The durable maintenance installation is `/Users/lamarso/.local/share/business-pstack`. No upstream code or blanket authority is imported automatically.
